    The Mathematics four-year planner and the Computer science four-year planner take what a student has completed and lay out the rest of the major quarter by quarter, with warnings for anything that cannot wait. Students can print a copy of the plan or copy a link to it. The Mathematics major map and Computer science major map show each major on one page, with every prerequisite drawn as an arrow. The Mathematics degree requirements and Computer science degree requirements sections restate the degree requirements from the 2026–27 Undergraduate Bulletin.
