    Section 6 Computer science degree requirements

    Restated from the Santa Clara University Undergraduate Bulletin 2026–27, Department of Mathematics and Computer Science.
    🔗

    Subsection 6.1 Major in Computer Science

    In addition to the Core Curriculum for the bachelor of science degree:
    🔗
    • MATH 11, 12, 13, 14, 51, 53.
      🔗
      🔗
    • CSCI 10, 60, 61, 62.
      🔗
      🔗
    • One of PHYS 31, CHEM 11, ENVS 21, or ENVS 23.
      🔗
      🔗
    • CSEN 20 and 20L, ECEN 21 and 21L.
      🔗
      🔗
    • MATH 122, CSCI 161 and 163, and CSEN 177 and 177L.
      🔗
      🔗
    • Five additional 4- or 5-unit upper-division courses in one of the four emphases below.
      🔗
      🔗
    At least four of the required upper-division courses must be taken at Santa Clara. A single upper-division course can satisfy only one major or minor requirement in the department. Students should decide on an emphasis by the end of junior year; only one emphasis is allowed, and Data Science may not be the emphasis for both a mathematics and a computer science major. Students, especially in the Software emphasis, are encouraged to take upper-division courses beyond the minimum, such as CSEN 178.
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection 6.2 Emphases

    Algorithms and Complexity
    🔗
    CSCI 162, CSCI 164; two more from CSCI 146, 147, 165, 181, MATH 101, 175, 176, 177, 178; one more from that list or any other 4–5 unit upper-division CSCI course below 190 or CSEN course below 188.
    🔗
    Data Science
    🔗
    CSCI 183, 184, 185; two more from CSCI 127, 146, 147, 164, 166, MATH 123, CSEN 166, or any other 4–5 unit upper-division CSCI course below 190 or CSEN course below 188.
    🔗
    Security
    🔗
    MATH 178, CSCI 180, CSCI 181; two more from MATH 175, CSEN 152 and 152L, CSEN 161 and 161L, CSEN 146 and 146L, or any other 4–5 unit upper-division CSCI course below 190 or CSEN course below 188, excluding CSEN 150.
    🔗
    Software
    🔗
    CSCI 169, CSCI 187, CSEN 146 and 146L; one more from CSCI 183, 180, 168, or any other 4–5 unit upper-division CSCI course below 190; one other from CSCI 183, 180, 168, CSEN 161, 178, or any other 4–5 unit upper-division CSCI course below 190 or CSEN course below 188.
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection 6.3 Minor in Computer Science

    CSCI 10, 60, 61, and 62; MATH 51; CSEN 20 and 20L; three 4- or 5-unit upper-division courses: two upper-division CSCI courses and one upper-division CSCI or CSEN course. CSCI 192 does not count toward the minor.
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection 6.4 Prerequisites used by the planner and the map

    From the course descriptions. Every CSCI prerequisite requires a grade of C− or better, or equivalent, or permission of the instructor where the Bulletin says so. “Alt.” marks courses offered in alternate years.
