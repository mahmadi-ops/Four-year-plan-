    Section 5 Mathematics degree requirements

    Restated from the Santa Clara University Undergraduate Bulletin 2026–27, Department of Mathematics and Computer Science. This section exists so that the planner’s rules can be checked against their source.
    🔗

    Subsection 5.1 Major in Mathematics

    In addition to the Core Curriculum for the bachelor of science degree:
    🔗
    • CSCI 10, or demonstrated equivalent proficiency in programming.
      🔗
      🔗
    • MATH 11, 12, 13, 14, 23, 51, 52, and 53.
      🔗
      🔗
    • PHYS 31 and 32. Students planning to teach in secondary schools may substitute PHYS 11 and 12 with approval of the department chair.
      🔗
      🔗
    • Seven approved 5-unit upper-division courses in mathematics (CSCI 162 also permitted), which must include
      🔗
      • at least one course in analysis: MATH 102, 105, or 153;
        🔗
        🔗
      • at least one course in algebra: MATH 103 or 111;
        🔗
        🔗
      • at least one course from geometry (MATH 101, 113, 174), discrete mathematics (MATH 176, 177), or applied mathematics (MATH 122, 125, 141, 144, 146, 155, 166).
        🔗
        🔗
      MATH 100 and courses numbered 190 and above do not count toward the seven.
      🔗
      🔗
    At least four of the required upper-division courses must be taken at Santa Clara. A single upper-division course can satisfy only one major or minor requirement in the department. Students should decide on an emphasis by the end of junior year; only one emphasis is allowed.
    🔗
    Students planning graduate study in pure mathematics should take MATH 105, 111, 112, 113, 153, and 154. Students planning graduate study in applied mathematics should complete the applied mathematics emphasis and take MATH 105, 144, 153, 154, and 155.
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection 5.2 Emphases

    Each emphasis adds the following to the requirements above.
    🔗
    Applied Mathematics
    🔗
    MATH 102, 122, 123; two of MATH 125, 141, 144, 146, 147, 155, 166, 178 (or an approved alternative); one of MATH 166, 147.
    🔗
    Data Science
    🔗
    MATH 122, 123; CSCI 10, 60, 61, 62, 183; CSCI 184 or CSEN 178; two of CSCI 127, 163, MATH 146, 147, 166, CSEN 166, 169, ECON 174.
    🔗
    Financial Mathematics
    🔗
    MATH 102, 122, 123, 125; two of MATH 144, 146, 147, 166; BUSN 70; ACTG 11, 12; FNCE 121, 124.
    🔗
    Mathematical Economics
    🔗
    MATH 102, 122, 123, 146, 147; ECON 113; two of MATH 125, 166, ECON 170–174.
    🔗
    Mathematics Education
    🔗
    MATH 101, 102, 122, 123 (or 8), 170, 175 (or 178), 195. The Urban Education minor is strongly advised.
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection 5.3 Minor in Mathematics

    MATH 11, 12, 13, 14, and either 52 or 53; three approved 5-unit upper-division mathematics courses. MATH 100, 192, and 195 do not count toward the minor.
    🔗
    🔗

    Subsection 5.4 Prerequisites used by the planner

    From the course descriptions. “Pre/co” means the course may be taken in the same quarter. “Alt.” marks courses the Bulletin lists as offered in alternate years.
